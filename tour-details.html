Your journey

Egypt, revealed at your pace.

This private adventure brings together Egypt’s greatest icons and its quieter, more personal moments. Expert guides, seamless transfers and hand-picked stays let you focus entirely on the experience.

Begin among Cairo’s pyramids and treasures before flying south to Luxor. Board your Nile cruise and drift between ancient temples, riverside villages and palm-lined landscapes. Finish with time to relax on Hurghada’s clear Red Sea coast.

Ancient wonders

Explore the Pyramids, Karnak, Valley of the Kings and Abu Simbel with a private licensed Egyptologist.

Nile in comfort

Sail from Luxor to Aswan aboard a carefully selected 5-star deluxe Nile cruise with all meals included.

Red Sea escape

Slow down with two restorative days beside Hurghada’s turquoise waters and pristine private beaches.

Day by day

A perfectly balanced itinerary.

Meet your private representative at Cairo International Airport with fast-track entry assistance through immigration and luggage handling. Private transfer in an air-conditioned luxury vehicle to your 5-star hotel in central Cairo or overlooking the Giza plateau.

5★ Luxury Hotel Cairo Welcome Dinner Private VIP Airport Transfer

Stand in awe before the Great Pyramid of Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure on the Giza Plateau. Visit the enigmatic Great Sphinx and the Valley Temple. Continue south to Saqqara to witness the Step Pyramid of Djoser, humanity's earliest monumental stone structure.

5★ Luxury Hotel Cairo Breakfast, Gourmet Egyptian Lunch Private Egyptologist Guide

Discover world-renowned royal treasures and King Tutankhamun's gold collection at the Grand Egyptian Museum and Museum of Egyptian Antiquities. Stroll through vibrant Khan el-Khalili bazaar before an evening short domestic flight to Luxor.

5★ Deluxe Nile Cruise Ship Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Domestic Flight Included

Embark on your 4-night luxury Nile Cruise sailing between Luxor and Aswan. Discover the monumental Karnak and Luxor Temples, the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut Temple, the falcon god temple in Edfu, and Kom Ombo. In Aswan, sail on a traditional felucca and explore Philae Temple.

5★ Deluxe Nile Cruise Full Board (All Meals Included) All Temple Entrance Fees

Private scenic transfer across the Eastern Desert to Hurghada on the Red Sea coast. Enjoy 3 days of total relaxation at a luxury beachfront resort. Optional activities include private snorkeling yacht charters, scuba diving, and sunset desert quad safaris.

5★ Beachfront Resort Hurghada All-Inclusive Dining Private Beach & Pool Access

Fly back to Cairo for a final day visiting the Saladin Citadel and Mosque of Muhammad Ali. Savor a celebratory farewell dinner with views of the Nile. On Day 13, enjoy private luxury transfer to Cairo International Airport for your return journey home.

5★ Luxury Hotel Cairo Breakfast, Farewell Dinner Private Airport Transfer
